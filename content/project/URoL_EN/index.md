URoL:EN is an NSF-funded project on regulatory functions and networks across biological and social systems. My research in this area uses historical records to study the evolution of legal and regulatory systems, including a century-scale dataset of the United States Legal Code published in *Scientific Data* (2026).

[Read the dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06313-w) · [Explore the data](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29540039)
